About

Built for people who are tired of “just delete the app” advice.

Quitting cold turkey sounds simple until it isn’t. Most people don’t need another lecture — they need a calmer path: notice the habit, understand the feed, try one useful swap at a time, and grow into different choices without shame.

Im Not Lazy is about changing the person gradually — how you spend attention, what you follow, and what you build with the hours you get back — not forcing an all-or-nothing detox overnight.

Recommendation controls

Tighten the feeds that steal your focus

Most platforms will not fully delete their algorithms — but almost all give you gentle levers: chronological feeds, interest resets, history pauses, and quieter recommendations. Pick a platform and try one or two changes at a time. The goal is gradual control, not an overnight detox.

Why this works

You’re not lazy. The feeds are engineered.

Variable rewards, infinite novelty, and notification urgency are product features — not personal failures. The point is gradual change in how you spend attention — not overnight phone lockdowns or shame.

See the cost in human terms

Three empty hours is not “just scrolling.” It can be two focused study blocks, a workout plus a walk, or a meaningful chunk of a side project. Naming the swap makes the habit visible — without judging today.

Control recommendations yourself

Switch to Following, reset suggested content, pause YouTube history, turn off TikTok personalization, sort Reddit by New. Small feed tweaks over time beat dramatic cold-turkey cuts.

Change the person, not the phone overnight

We focus on awareness and better choices session by session. Try following something useful next time. Progress compounds when you grow the habit — not when you punish yourself for today.
