Recommendation controls

Tighten the feeds that steal your focus

Most platforms will not fully delete their algorithms — but almost all give you levers: chronological feeds, interest resets, history pauses, time limits, and notification cuts. Pick a platform and choose the controls that fit how you actually use it.

Phone-level levers that work on every app

Why this works

You’re not lazy. The feeds are engineered.

Variable rewards, infinite novelty, and notification urgency are product features — not personal failures. Small friction and clearer tradeoffs beat shame and cold-turkey advice.

See the cost in human terms

Three empty hours is not “just scrolling.” It can be two focused study blocks, a workout plus a walk, or a meaningful chunk of a side project. Naming the swap makes the habit visible.

Control recommendations yourself

Switch to Following, reset suggested content, pause YouTube history, turn off TikTok personalization, sort Reddit by New. Multiple options mean you can tighten without quitting overnight.

Add friction, keep agency

Remove icons, mute badges, set timers, charge the phone outside the bedroom. You’re not banned from the apps — you’re choosing when they get access to you.
